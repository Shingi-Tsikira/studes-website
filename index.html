Why Studes?

Finding student accommodation always seems to happen at the worst possible time. Studes gives students a structured way to search and compare rooms, and gives landlords a direct line to the exact audience they are trying to reach.

How It Works

  1. 1

    Browse

    Filter rooms by type, price and area on the Listings page, or look through the photo gallery first.

  2. 2

    Enquire

    Send one enquiry and get an instant cost estimate and a reference number for your records.

  3. 3

    Move in

    We confirm availability with the landlord and you move in close to campus, with a deposit and fees that are clear from the start.
