Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I hear back after sending an enquiry?

A Studes team member replies within two working days. Your reference number on the confirmation page helps us find your enquiry quickly.

Do I pay anything to send an enquiry?

No. Enquiries are free. The estimate you see is only a guide; the once-off booking fee and deposit are only due once a landlord confirms your room.

How much deposit will I need?

A refundable deposit equal to one month of rent. The enquiry form calculates it for you.

I am a landlord — how does Studes earn money?

Landlords pay a small commission on rent: 5% for a standard listing and 8% for a featured listing that appears first.

Which campuses do you cover?

Wits University and the University of Johannesburg in Johannesburg, and the University of Pretoria in Hatfield.
