About Studes

A simple, trustworthy way for students and landlords to find each other.

Our Story

Most students end up finding accommodation through informal means — a friend of a friend, a Facebook group, a flyer on a noticeboard. That leaves very little way to verify who they are dealing with, compare options properly, or know what is actually available near campus. Studes was created to fix that by giving both students and landlords a proper, structured place to list, search, and communicate.

Our Mission

To connect students and landlords in one trustworthy platform, replacing scattered, unreliable channels with a single, structured experience built around what each side actually needs to do.

Our Vision

To become the first place students and landlords turn to when searching for or listing accommodation near tertiary campuses.

Who We Serve

  • Students looking for verified, comparable accommodation options close to campus.
  • Landlords who want a direct, reliable channel to reach student tenants.

What We Stand For

  • Transparency: prices, deposits and fees are shown up front, with a cost estimate before you commit.
  • Safety: listings show security features such as access control and well-lit areas.
  • Convenience: everything is organised by distance to campus, so you spend less time commuting.

Where We Operate

Studes serves students around Wits University (Braamfontein), the University of Johannesburg (Auckland Park) and the University of Pretoria (Hatfield). See our offices on the map, browse the photo gallery, or send an enquiry.
